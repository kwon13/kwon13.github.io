\sectionheading{Profile}
I began my career as an ML Researcher at Glorang at the age of 19 and later worked at Selectstar. I am now a Research Intern at Yonsei University's MLAI Lab, studying self-evolving systems. I explore ``Expansion'', a framework in which AI models learn to redefine and restructure their own task specifications through real-world interactions.

\sectionheading{Research Interests}
Small language models and efficient reasoning; open-ended self-evolving LLMs; meta-learning architectures; adaptive evaluation.
